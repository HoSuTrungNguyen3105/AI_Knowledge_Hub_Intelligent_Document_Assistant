What the diagram follows

docker-compose.ymlruns frontend, backend, and MongoDB services. The browser talks to /api/v1; regular requests use Axios, while chat uses fetch for SSE streaming.

Provider keys are decrypted server-side by backend/app/core/credential_vault.py; the frontend receives streamed answers, not keys.
